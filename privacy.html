Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026
The short version: Haptic Stopwatch has no account, no servers, and no analytics. Your timers and settings live on your own Apple Watch and iPhone (and your private iCloud, if you enable it). We never collect, see, or sell your data.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Haptic Stopwatch app for Apple Watch and its iPhone companion app (together, the “App”). It explains what information the App handles and how.

Information we collect

None. The App does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal information to work. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Information the App stores on your device

To do its job, the App saves your configuration locally on your devices. This may include:

  • Timers, sequences, and settings — the names, durations, intervals, haptic patterns, and options (beeps, announce, etc.) you create.
  • App preferences — your in-app choices, such as the last mode used.

This information stays on your Apple Watch and iPhone. It is shared between your own watch and phone over Apple’s Watch Connectivity, which is a direct device-to-device channel — it does not pass through us.

iCloud

If your device is set up to back up app data to iCloud, your timers and settings may be included in that backup. iCloud is your private Apple account; this data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it.

System permissions

The App uses a small number of Apple system capabilities:

  • Haptics to tap your wrist, and an optional extended runtime session to keep firing with the screen off.
  • Audio for optional beeps and spoken announcements.
  • Notifications to deliver a cue if the watch is on silent.
  • Siri & Shortcuts to run Start / Pause / Reset actions you trigger.

These are processed on-device by the operating system. The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, health data, or microphone recordings.

Information we share

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your information, because we never collect it in the first place.

Children’s privacy

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

You can delete any timer or sequence inside the App at any time. Deleting the App from your devices removes its locally stored data. To remove anything kept in iCloud, manage your iCloud storage in the Apple Settings app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on our GitHub feedback repository.
